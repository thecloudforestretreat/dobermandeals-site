Doberman owner stories: share an experience thoughtfully

Interested in sharing life with your Doberman? Start a conversation about your account, privacy and permission before sending a story or photographs.

By Doberman Deals · Updated October 13, 2026

An invitation to share

Tell us which everyday experience you would like to discuss: settling into a new home, learning a routine or finding useful support. Start with a short description of the topic through our contact page. There are no published owner accounts in this collection yet; we are inviting contributions.

Decide what you are comfortable sharing

Before sending a full account, discuss the name or attribution you want to use and any details you want kept private. Do not include private addresses, medical records or other people’s personal information in your first message. Photographs or accounts involving other people need appropriate permission; owning a dog does not give permission to publish someone else’s words or image.

Agree on the account before publication

A contribution needs a real owner’s account, clarification of relevant details and an agreed version for publication. Tell us about sponsorship, gifts or a relationship with a service or brand mentioned. Permission to discuss a story is separate from permission to publish text, photographs or a translated version. No submission is published automatically.

Read experience in its own context

A personal account describes what happened for one dog and household. It does not establish a result for every Doberman, prove that a product works or replace individual veterinary or training advice. A published story should make its context and any commercial connection clear.

Common questions

Can I send someone else’s story or photographs?

Contact us about permissions first. Do not copy private material or assume someone else agreed to publication.

Does contacting you give permission to publish?

No. Discuss the account, attribution, media and any translation separately before publication.
