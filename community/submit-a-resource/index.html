Suggest a useful Doberman resource

Share a public primary link and explain how it helps Doberman owners. Suggestions go for review; sending one does not publish a listing or endorsement.

By Doberman Deals · Updated October 13, 2026

What to include

Send the organization or resource name, its public primary URL, the area or audience it serves and the practical reason for suggesting it. Resources may include rescue organizations, training support, accessible dog spaces or source material for a guide. Say if you own the service or have a commercial connection.

Keep the submission focused

Use your email for a possible follow-up and public information about the resource. Do not include medical records, payment details, passwords, private home addresses or other people’s personal contact details. If the resource concerns an individual’s story or photographs, contact us about permission first rather than uploading or pasting private material.

What review means

Suggestions are requests for review, not automatic listings. We may check the primary website, service scope, source quality and any disclosed commercial relationship, or ask for clarification. Acceptance is not guaranteed. A future listing should identify what was checked and its limits; it does not certify safety, credentials or availability.

If you are reporting a change

Identify the existing guide or listing and the public source showing the change. A clear correction is more useful than a copied directory or promotional paragraph. Use the form below or our contact page to send a focused note.

Common questions

Will my suggestion appear immediately?

No. The form sends a suggestion for review and does not create a public listing.

Can I submit someone else’s private details or photos?

Send public primary links. Contact us about permission before sharing personal stories or media.
