Before your puppy arrives

Choose a resting area and a toilet route. Check gates, cables, small objects and cleaning supplies. Ask the breeder or rescue for the current food, routine and records so you can discuss changes with your veterinarian rather than guess.

The first week is about settling in

Keep introductions brief and give your puppy an easy way to retreat. Plan household cover around toilet opportunities and rest. Record what actually happens; that is more useful than expecting your puppy to follow a perfect timetable.

Teach everyday skills gently

Name response, voluntary entry into a resting space and comfortable handling can begin as easy games. Choose small goals and keep practice comfortable. Ask your veterinarian about local disease risks and suitable exposure opportunities before planning outings.
