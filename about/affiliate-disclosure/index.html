Affiliate disclosure

You should be able to understand when a recommendation has a commercial relationship.

By Doberman Deals · Updated October 12, 2026

Commercial links

If a link earns us a commission, that relationship should be identified near the recommendation. A commission does not establish that a product suits your dog.

What these guides contain

The first puppy and training guides offer selection criteria and practical examples rather than ranked product reviews. They do not claim hands-on testing of particular brands.

Ask about a recommendation

If a relationship or claim is unclear, contact us with the page address and the link you mean. Read product instructions and verify current specifications before choosing equipment.
