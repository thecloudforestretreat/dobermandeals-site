Privacy notice

Here is how website visits, email contact and optional waitlist signups are handled.

By Doberman Deals · Updated October 12, 2026

Website measurement

The public website uses Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager and Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and interactions. Our preview website excludes the production Google tracking setup. Google and Cloudflare may process technical information under their own policies. We do not send signup email addresses or free-text dog-care answers in our analytics events.

Optional launch updates

The existing waitlist stores an email address, agreement to launch updates and the signup date in Cloudflare. Turnstile helps protect the form against automated abuse. A signup is for updates, not a purchase. You can email us to request removal or stop receiving updates.

Email and your choices

Information you include in an email is used to handle the request. Send only what is necessary. Contact dobermandeals3@gmail.com for questions or requests about your information. Browser privacy settings and the providers’ policies give additional information about their services.
