Understanding our review process

Look at the sources and the scope of a guide before applying it to your own dog.

By Doberman Deals · Updated October 12, 2026

General guidance and professional review

An educational guide cannot replace an assessment by your veterinarian or an appropriate behavior professional. A professional review, where shown, should identify the reviewer and the relevant credentials. Do not assume that every page has received that review.

What an updated date means

An updated date describes a change to the page. It does not guarantee that every linked resource remains current or that guidance suits an individual dog. Check the original source and ask for professional input when your situation is different.

Help us improve a page

Point out unclear advice, inaccessible content or a source that has changed. Include the page address and enough detail to explain the issue. Our contact page provides the route for feedback.
