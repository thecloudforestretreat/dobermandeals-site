Report a correction

Found a factual error, a broken link or confusing advice? Send the page address, the passage and what you think needs changing through our contact form.

By Doberman Deals · Updated October 13, 2026

Send enough detail to check

Choose “Content correction” in the contact form. Include the page URL and quote the relevant sentence. Explain the problem and add a source link if you have one. For a sizing or price issue, include the product model, seller and date you checked. Leave out medical records, payment details and other people’s personal information.

How a correction should be assessed

The correction process starts with the reported passage and its supporting source. A changed manufacturer specification calls for checking the current document; a health claim may need veterinary review. A report is a request for assessment, rather than an automatic change. We do not promise a response deadline.

What a clear change note records

A substantive correction should identify the page, the date, the information changed and the evidence supporting the change. The format below shows those fields. It is a proposed format, not a record of past corrections. Minor spelling fixes need not imply that every claim on a page has been rechecked.

Common questions

Can I report a problem in Spanish?

Yes. Use the Spanish contact form and include the Spanish page address so we can identify the passage.

Is this a place to request urgent care advice?

No. Contact your veterinarian or an emergency veterinary provider for urgent care.

Suggested correction-note format

Date
The date the substantive correction was made.
Page and passage
The address and information affected.
Change and evidence
What changed and which source supports it.
