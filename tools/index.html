Choose the right record

The puppy checklist tracks preparations you have completed. The training log records a specific skill, setting and observable response. The care planner lets you enter a task and a date. None of these tools assesses your dog’s health or diagnoses behavior.

Keep a copy when you need one

Checks, notes and dates remain in the open page and reset when you leave or reload. Print your completed page to retain a copy. The planner does not send reminders or create calendar appointments.

Use guidance alongside the tool

A recorded task is useful only when it matches your dog’s needs. Follow the linked puppy and training guides for context, and ask your veterinarian about individual medical schedules. These tools organize information; professional care decisions still need the appropriate advice.

Common questions

Are my notes uploaded?

These tools keep entries in the open page. They do not save or send the text.

Will the planner notify me?

No. It is an editable, printable record; it does not send notifications.
